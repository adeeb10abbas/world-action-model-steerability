C--D uses the same matching rule and measures the specified construction change, including ``Put'' versus ``Place \ldots so that,'' rather than syntax or length in isolation. LAT and DIST require released placements directly on the table. Removing DIST pedestals is scene cleanup, not a measured support ablation. Support presence is not a study factor. Differences in references, geometry and placement requirements preclude a pure-axis causal claim across families; cross-family success differences are descriptive.

\subsection{Prediction measurement}
Before scoring fidelity, establish each generated frame's physical target time and view inside the prefix actually executed before replanning. Record actual model-input arrays and their crop/packing transforms. A presentation frame rate or an available camera is insufficient. Freeze the positive horizon $H$ on development, separately for N3, E3 and F3, and select requests at indices $\lfloor0.25(n-1)\rfloor$ and $\lfloor0.75(n-1)\rfloor$, deduplicating when necessary.

For each normalized target--anchor relative image coordinate $u$, report
\begin{align}
E_{\mathrm{prediction}} &= \lVert u_{\mathrm{pred}}(H)-u_{\mathrm{actual}}(H)\rVert_2,\\
E_{\mathrm{persistence}} &= \lVert u_{\mathrm{current}}-u_{\mathrm{actual}}(H)\rVert_2,\\
\mathrm{skill} &= E_{\mathrm{persistence}}-E_{\mathrm{prediction}}.
\end{align}
Two blinded raters localize cube and anchor centers; discrepancies above 0.02 image diagonals require adjudication. DIST includes both anchors. Localization, adjudication and persistence exports remain to be completed; categorical labels alone are insufficient.

Validate semantic labels with 50 offline images per family: 20 balanced unambiguous development states, 20 balanced unambiguous held-out states, and 10 ambiguous/occluded variants. Require at least 19/20 correct held-out signs and no forced labels on ambiguous states. World height and Euclidean distance cannot be inferred automatically from pixel coordinates. At matching $H$, report both consistent, prediction-only consistent, execution-only consistent, both inconsistent and unknown. Preserve coverage and missingness bounds; a failed prediction measurement does not erase valid execution evidence.

\subsection{Analysis completion}
The compiler now distinguishes physical separation $r(+)-r(-)=M(+)+M(-)$ in metres from success asymmetry $S(+)-S(-)$. Observed manipulation failures retain their continuous endpoints; missing or censored endpoints have unavailable separation. The final export must combine the I--C and C--D effects, per-goal transitions, physical separation, stage diagnostics, prediction skill and coverage, exposing complete layouts out of 24 for every model-family branch.

Retain the registered 20,000 layout bootstrap draws, 100,000 paired sign flips, six-test Holm correction, censoring rules and two-endpoint equivalence criteria. Nonsignificance is not equivalence. Descriptive bootstrap intervals alone cannot support a positive equivalence claim; all-zero or otherwise zero-variance layout contrasts are inconclusive pending a valid finite-sample procedure fixed before confirmation. Freeze stationary/moving handling at 0.02 image diagonals and declare first/later-request and alternative-supported-$H$ sensitivity analyses before confirmation. End-to-end outputs and the prediction-annotation workflow still require validation with labeled engineering fixtures; those checks are not experimental results.
